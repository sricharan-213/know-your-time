# 💫 Age & Date Calculator (Interpreter Edition)

Ever wondered how many **days, hours, or even seconds** you've lived on this planet? Or what weekday your birthday falls on every year? Maybe even get a little astrological vibe for that special day? Well, this simple Python project does just that – and more!

## 📌 What It Does
- Takes **two dates** from you (start and end).
- Checks if the dates are valid (yep, even leap years).
- Tells you the **day of the week** for your birthdays through the years.
- Gives you a **cool astrology message** based on the first birthday’s weekday 🌟.
- Calculates how long you've lived – in **days, hours, minutes, and seconds**!

## 🎯 Why I Made This
Honestly? Just out of pure curiosity and fun. I was learning Python and thought:
> "Wouldn’t it be cool to build something that actually *talks back* to you about your life in numbers?"

So here it is – a little personal, a little nerdy, and totally worth sharing!

## 🛠 Tech Used
- Python (Interpreter-based input/output)
- No external libraries – just pure Python love 💻❤️
